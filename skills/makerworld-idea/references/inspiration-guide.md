# MakerWorld inspiration sources

Where to look on MakerWorld for ideas, and what each page shows. The Creator Center pages need the user's signed-in browser. Layouts change: if a page looks different from this, read it and adapt, and update this file. Seen November 2026.

## Creator Center › Inspiration › Popular Searches

`https://makerworld.com/en/my/creator-center/popular-searches`

- A plain list of about 45 search terms from the last 7 days, most searched first. No counts.
- Many terms aren't English (Portuguese and Spanish show up a lot: natal, chaveiro, suporte celular, presépio, navidad, papai noel). Translate them and group the ones that mean the same thing ("natal", "navidad", "christmas", "noel" are one theme).
- Mixed in are brand and character terms (pokemon, harry potter, spiderman, star wars, batman, lego, grinch), printer and part names (x2d, spool, filament clip, benchy), and filters ("no ams" means people want single-colour models).

## Creator Center › Inspiration › Trends

`https://makerworld.com/en/my/creator-center/trends`

Shows a "Data as of <date>" line near the top. Always report that date: it can be weeks old.

| Section | What it shows | How to use it |
|---|---|---|
| Platform Campaigns | Running campaigns and contests (for example "Laser & Cut Creator Rewards: +50% points", themed contests with their top prize) | Contest leads (follow the link into the contest route) and bonus-point categories |
| Hot Searches | A short ranked list of searches (around 8) | The strongest demand signals; check each one against supply |
| Earn with BOM Kits | Parts-list order and sales change over 7 days | Context only |
| Trending Parts / Trending Filaments (tabs) | Ranked Maker's Supply parts with their store code (for example "LED Lamp Kit (1PCS) - MH001", "M3x5x4 Round Threaded Brass Heat-Insert Nut (20PCS) - AB012", "D6x2 mm Round Magnet (20PCS) - CA004"), 7-day order change and share | Which hardware buyers are already ordering, so ideas built around it earn parts-list commission |
| Creator Spotlight | Top sellers and rising creators | What style is working (flexis, desk companions, mecha kits) |
| Recommended BOM Ideas | Models MakerWorld is promoting that use a parts list | What a parts-list model looks like; most use an LED kit or tealight |
| What's new | New Maker's Supply kits, with prices | Fresh kits with few models built around them yet |
| Top Opportunities | Maker's Supply kits with strong recent sales (for example Clock Components Kit MH011, Magnetic Levitation DIY Kit ZC008, Dynamic Installation Art Kit ZH046) | The best commission leads: design a model around one of these kits |

The parts tables and kit rows load as you scroll and don't always come through as page text. Scroll to them and take a screenshot, or read the section's text directly.

## Commission incentive (parts-list commission)

`https://makerworld.com/en/my/creator-center/incentive/application`

- A creator adds Bambu Store items (Maker's Supply kits and parts, filaments, and laser materials) to a model's Bill of Materials section. When someone buys through it, the creator earns cash commission on the order.
- Access is switched on automatically within 1 to 2 business days once the creator meets **any one** of: 500 or more followers; at least 1 CyberBrick model uploaded; $100 or more in parts-list sales in the last 12 months; 20 or more parts-list orders in the last 12 months. Orders before access is switched on earn nothing, and there's no way to see progress until then.
- MakerWorld recommends adding a parts list to every model with hardware, even before access, because it makes the model easier to rebuild.
- Maker's Supply product pages give exact dimensions and downloadable 3D files of each part, so a model can be designed around the real part.

## Searching MakerWorld (demand against supply)

`https://makerworld.com/en/search/models?keyword=<term>` (URL-encode the term)

- The tabs show the result count: "Models (999+)", plus Laser & Cut models, collections and posts. 999+ means crowded.
- **Discover more** lists related searches (for example "christmas paper towel holder", "under-cabinet", "skadis paper towel"). These are long-tail ideas that are often less crowded.
- Each result card shows the name, the creator and two counts. Read which is which from the card's icons rather than assuming. Some cards carry a "Maker's Supply" badge, meaning they have a parts list.
- Sort options: Relevance, Trending, Boosts, Newest, Downloads, Likes. Sort by Downloads for the proven leaders and by Newest to see what's being made right now.

How to judge a term:

- **Strong opening:** plenty of searches (on Popular Searches or Hot Searches) but a low result count, or a high count where the top results are old, plain or poorly photographed.
- **Crowded:** 999+ results with several polished leaders in the tens of thousands of downloads. Only worth it with a clear twist (a mechanism, a fit for a specific product, a style the leaders lack).
- **Seasonal:** holiday terms peak around their date. Count back the time to design, print, photograph and publish, and say whether it's still worth it.
