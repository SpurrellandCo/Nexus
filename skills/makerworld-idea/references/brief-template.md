## What it is

## Who it's for and why

The person who'd download it and the problem it solves for them. For inspiration ideas, which searches it answers.

## Requirements

Must-haves, one per line. Each should be checkable on the finished print (for example "holds a standard 280 mm kitchen roll", "prints without supports", "fits the A1 mini plate").

## Colours and printing

Single or multi-colour (and which colours), filament type, print orientation, supports, rough print time, plate-size limits.

## Contest

Only if it's a contest entry: link, theme in one line, deadline (and days left when written), how it's judged, and every rule that limits the design (multi-colour, renders allowed, exclusivity, licence).

## Market check

Similar models found on MakerWorld (name, creator, likes/downloads, what they do well or badly), the gap this idea fills, and the search terms it answers. "Not checked" is fine for an own idea where the user skipped it.

## Listing draft

- **Working name** (50 characters at most):
- **One-line pitch:**
- **Category:** the closest MakerWorld category, as the site words it (for example Tools › Organizers or Household › Kitchen). For inspiration and contest ideas, use the category the leading similar models are in.

## Scores

Theme or demand fit, standout, printability, buildability in Fusion, commission potential: each out of 5, with one line of reasoning.
